### **Collision Domain**
 A network segment where devices share a physical medium; if two devices transmit simultaneously, their signals collide and corrupt.
### **Broadcast Domain**
A network boundary where a broadcast message (such as an ARP request) sent by any device is received by all devices within that same scope.
