We classify networks based on their geographical coverage.
## PAN (Personal Area Network
A personal area network is a computer network for interconnecting electronic devices within an individual person's workspace.
#### Key Points
- Very small range coverage
- Mostly personal devices used like phone, digital watch, computer etc
- It can be wired as well wirelless so we called WPAN.
#### Technologies
NFC, Bluetooth, USB, Personal Hotspot

## LAN (Local Area Network)
A network that connect limited/local geographical area in our home, office is called a local area network
#### Key Points
- It's range is more than PAN.
- It can be Ethernet/Wifi.
- LAN provide high speed to communicate to other devices.
- It can be sharing data in network
#### Technologies
WIFI, Ethernet

## MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)
It's coverage more than LAN and mostly cover City/Campuses
#### Point Keys
- Coverage the different location of city/campus.
- MAN is formed by connecting multiple Local Area Networks (LANs) within a larger geographic area

## WAN (Wide Area Network)
It's connect large geographical area like different continents, different provinces, different countries
#### Key Points
- It larger network, it coverage large geographical area.
- Internet is the biggest example of WAN.
- WAN is formed by connecting multiple Local Area Networks (LANs) and Metropolitan Area Networks (MANs) within a larger geographic area
