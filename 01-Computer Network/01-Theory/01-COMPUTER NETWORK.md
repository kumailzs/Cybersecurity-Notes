## Computer Network
When two and more than computer connect each other to send and receive data is called **Computer Network**
### Why It's Important
Computer Networking is used everyday in our life like we use phone to call, watching videos on youtube, search something in google, use social media to connect with friends and etc
### Key Points
-  Connection should be through wire (Fiber Cable, Coaxial, and etc) or wireless (Wifi, Bluetooth, and etc).
-  **Internet** is also a giant network, there's billions of devices connect each other to send and receive data.
### For Example
```text
Phone → Router → YouTube
Laptop → Printer
Phone → Signal Tower → Phone
```
